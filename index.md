## Welcome to [!INCLUDE[prod_long](includes/prod_long.md)]

[!INCLUDE[prod_short](includes/prod_short.md)] is the industry standard for logistics service providers. Used by 3PL leaders around the globe, the software automates and streamlines logistics processes. Highly adaptable and rich in functionality, the software enables you to manage your business, including finance, warehousing, transportation, forwarding, and more. 3PL Dynamics is fast to implement, easy to configure, and simplicity guides innovations in product design, development, implementation, and usability.

On this page, you can access a range of resources to help you stay up to date with the latest industry trends and best practices. This includes information regarding what's new and changes within our modules but also information regarding your upcoming update or how to be prepared before you start working with 3pl Dynamics.

In the navigation panel at the top, you can find all the available categories. It is separated into our main modules: Warehousing, Transportation, Forwarding, Customs, and Integrations. Besides those categories, there is a category Generic for all other information that does not fit or goes across multiple categories. The IT Operations has all the necessary information for technical questions, installations, and updates.
